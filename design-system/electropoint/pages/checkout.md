# Checkout overrides

- No mega-menu, no 3D, no marketing carousels, no animation beyond focus/validation
- Progress: 6 labeled steps, current step only is interactive
- Desktop: form left, order summary sticky right
- Mobile: summary collapsed `<details>` above pay CTA
- Payment logos as text + official marks, not decorative illustrations
- Errors: `role="alert"` with message, not color-only
